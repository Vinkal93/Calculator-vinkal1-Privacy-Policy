Privacy Policy — Calculator Vinkal

Last Updated: November 13, 2025

Core Functionality

  • Basic Calculator
  • Currency Calculator
  • Unit Converter
  • Discount Calculator
  • Percentage Calculator
  • GST Calculator
  • Date Calculator
  • Health Calculator
  • Loan Calculator

The app also includes a Calculation History feature, stored locally on your device.

Customization

  • Themes: Choose from various color themes saved locally.
  • Startup Calculator: Select your preferred calculator at app launch.
  • Keep Screen On: Option to prevent screen timeout while using the app.

Data Handling

  • Local Storage: All app data is stored using SharedPreferences.
  • No Personal Information: The app does not collect or share PII.
  • No Server-Side Storage: All operations occur on your device.
  • Permissions: No special permissions required.

Third-Party Libraries

  • Gson: Used for serializing and deserializing calculation history for local storage only.
Important:

All data remains stored on your device. You may clear your calculation history anytime from the app settings. No data is transmitted externally.

Calculator Vinkal — Local-first calculator with full privacy. No data collection, no tracking.
